// MSc Thesis · TU Delft · Grade 9/10

Affordable, Modular, Flexible Endoscope System

Full mechanical development of a modular flexible colonoscope for low-resource clinical settings. Designed the internal architecture in SolidWorks to integrate a tendon-driven four-way bending section, fluidic routing (suction, irrigation, insufflation), and a modular handle-cassette assembly within a compact 419g prototype. Fabricated from commercial off-the-shelf components and additive manufacturing. Validated articulation exceeding 210° in all four steering planes (peak: 214°) across repeated test cycles. Built at a fraction of the cost of commercial systems.

SolidWorks DFMEA DfM/DfA Prototype Fabrication Functional Validation Fluidic Systems ISO 13485

// Precision Mechanism Design · ESA

Compliant Planar Joint — ESA Microvibration Isolation

MATLAB-based constrained optimisation of folded leaf spring geometry for a spacecraft hexapod. Achieved near-zero stiffness in 3 planar DOF and maximum stiffness in 3 constrained directions under strict mass and height limits. COMSOL FEM validation across 6 degrees of freedom.

COMSOL FEM MATLAB Optimisation SolidWorks Compliant Mechanisms

// Surgical Instrument Design

Steerable Pen Camera — RAIS Surgical Device

Designed a miniaturised steerable pen camera attachment for gasless laparoscopic surgery in low/middle-income countries. Miniaturised kinematic linkages within surgical constraints, optimised for additive manufacturing. Assembled and validated integration with surgical retractor hardware.

SolidWorks Kinematic Linkages 3D Printing Surgical Devices

// Assistive Robotics · Mechatronics

Assistive Feeding Robot for MS Patients

3-DOF telescopic arm with Maxon BLDC motors for Multiple Sclerosis patients. Dual-stage tremor damping: passive viscous damping + adaptive PID/notch filter. Full CAD architecture and mechatronic control block diagram enabling seamless mode switching.
